About Us

Incorporated in 2022 Pacific Workforce is a PAN India staffing and manpower solutions firm based in Nashik, Maharashtra. Our commitment to excellence and competitive pricing has propelled us to foster strong partnerships with renowned companies worldwide. Focusing on quality and efficiency, we offer comprehensive manpower outsourcing, placement, compliance management, and HR services.
